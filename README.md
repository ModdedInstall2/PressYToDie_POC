Press Y To Die (PROOF OF CONCEPT)​
----------------------------------

This game has basically been my dream to make for a very long time now, and here I am finally making it! Yay :)This is the proof of concept for my metafictional 2D puzzle platformer, Press Y To Die. The core mechanic is that by pressing Y, you die - your ghost separates from your body for ten seconds until you respawn, and during that time, you can move through walls and interact with certain objects. Keep in mind that this is just a proof-of-concept - if I do make this into a full game (which I really, really want to), it probably won't keep much content from this.​

### Featuring:​

*  18 brain-bending levels

*  One of the most unique puzzle mechanics I know of

*  Glitches, wonky collision, and crummy physics galore

*  Hopefully a pretty fun time!​

### How To Play

_All keybindings are for Xbox controllers. Other controllers have the same bindings based on where they are on the button pad. If you must play on a keyboard, it's R, Z, X, C, and Y, in that order._

The goal of each level is to activate the button(s) that will open the exit door, and you have a pretty useful moveset to accomplish that.

Press Select to restart levels.

Press A to jump. You have a double jump!

Press B to pick up cubes. (Yes, they're called cubes in a 2D game. Sue me.)

Press X to transfer cubes to/from the ethereal plane.

Press Y... to die.

When you're dead, you can go through any white walls, and you're also able to float around freely. Also: remember how I mentioned the ethereal plane? Well, you can't interact with those cubes when you're alive, but you _can_ carry them around when you're dead! The opposite is true for cubes on our plane of existence. In addition, you leave a corpse behind when you die, and this corpse can actually hold down buttons! Isn't that awesome? Of course, it vanishes once you respawn, because you kinda need a body to be alive - but still!

There are also some ways to actually die, though. Like, you-need-to-restart-the-level die. For one, you have water. This stuff will dissolve your body and destroy your soul. Why does it do that? I dunno. Maybe it's caustic holy water or something?

There are also turrets, of which there are two kinds. One kind is designed to eliminate all supernatural activity, and will send you plummeting to the ground when you're in ghost form. The other kind is military-grade, and as we all know, the military only prepares for supernatural activity in a small bunker in Nevada. However, all the bullets it shoots are really wet for some reason. Probably just an early prototype.​

Anyway, have fun, and please let me know what you think of it!

### Known Bugs​

Whenever you start a new level, there's a small chance you will be unable to interact with objects. This goes away when you enter water or another form of death zone, so to assist in testing, a ModdedInstall2™ High-Volatility Emergency Death Zone™ has been added to the start of each level. Simply walk backwards once a level loads or you restart it and the level will reset, allowing you to complete it as normal! (And don't worry, I've made sure you can't abuse this to cheat levels.)

The turrets are kind of really, really bad at their job (especially the ones that shoot you when you're alive). I probably need to fire them. However, they're far too good at it when you jump on them at a precise angle - so... don't jump on them at a precise angle.

There is technically a saving/loading feature, but it's kind of hit-or-miss, so I'd advise against relying on it.

Are these bugs technically fixable? Yes. Do they directly interfere with your ability to play the game? Yes. Do I have the motivation to fix them after I spent two weeks refactoring how the game loads levels because it broke literally an hour before the SAGE deadline earlier this month? No. Not one bit.​
